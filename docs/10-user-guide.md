# 10. End-User Manual & Workflow Guide

Welcome to **Tuntas**! This guide walks you through every module of the platform to maximize your daily productivity, project organization, habit discipline, and learning mastery.

---

## 1. Getting Started & Account Setup
1. **Sign Up**: Navigate to `/register`, enter your Full Name, Email, and a secure password.
2. **Sign In**: Access your workspace at `/login`.
3. **Preferences**: Go to **Settings** (`/settings`) from the left sidebar to set your timezone (e.g. `Asia/Jakarta`, `UTC`), select your appearance theme (Dark/Light), and update your display credentials.

---

## 2. Navigating the Personal Dashboard
When you log in, the **Dashboard** (`/dashboard`) provides a holistic summary of your day:
- **Metrics Bar**: Check your active tasks count, completion rate, active projects, and habit streaks.
- **7-Day Velocity Chart**: Visual bar chart illustrating how many tasks you finalized over the past week.
- **Today's Priorities**: Direct checklist of tasks scheduled for today. Click the circle to complete them directly.
- **Active Projects & Goals**: Real-time progress bars showing completion percentages.
- **Upcoming Examinations**: Countdown badges alerting you to upcoming tests and certification milestones.

---

## 3. Advanced Task Management (`/tasks`)
The task manager helps you organize items with professional agility:
- **Kanban Board**: Drag or move cards between four status columns:
  - `To-Do`: New actions queued.
  - `On Progress`: Work in flight.
  - `In Review`: Finished work awaiting verification.
  - `Completed`: Done.
- **Adding a Task**: Click the blue **+ New Task** button in the header. Enter Title, Description, Priority (`Low`, `Medium`, `High`, `Urgent`), Due Date, Category, and any checklist subtasks.
- **Subtasks Progress**: As you check off subtasks inside a task card, the progress bar fills dynamically (e.g., `3/5`).
- **Recurring Tasks**: Enable recurrence (`Daily`, `Weekly`, `Monthly`). When marked completed, Tuntas keeps the historical record and automatically generates the next scheduled instance.

---

## 4. Project Management (`/projects`)
Projects group related tasks into strategic initiatives:
- **Create Project**: Specify Project Name, Description, Accent Color, and Target Deadline.
- **Automatic Progress Tracking**: The progress bar updates automatically as you complete tasks assigned to the project.
- **Milestones**: Break down major deliverables into sequential milestones to guarantee steady delivery.

---

## 5. Unified Calendar (`/calendar`)
The Calendar is your consolidated agenda:
- Displays task deadlines, project targets, scheduled examination dates, and logged habits in one monthly view.
- Click any calendar date cell to inspect all events scheduled for that day.
- Easily toggle between months using the navigation arrows.

---

## 6. Habit Tracker & Consistency Engine (`/habits`)
Build lasting daily habits with gamified streak mechanics:
- **Check-in**: Click the checkmark icon on any habit card to record today's completion.
- **Streaks**: Watch your `Current Streak` increment day by day. Try beating your `Best Streak`!
- **Freeze Tokens**: If you miss a day due to illness or travel, use a **Freeze Token** to protect your continuous streak from resetting to zero.

---

## 7. Goals & OKRs (`/goals`)
Transform high-level ambitions into measurable achievements:
- **Define OKRs**: Set goal titles (e.g., *"Read 12 Software Engineering Books"*), target values (`12`), and measurement units (`Books`).
- **Add Key Milestones**: Break your goal into incremental checkpoints.
- **Live Progress**: As you update milestone numbers, the overall goal percentage adjusts automatically.

---

## 8. Learning Hub (LMS) (`/learning`)
Organize your self-study and academic coursework:
- **Subjects & Modules**: Group topics into structured courses (e.g., *Full-Stack Systems*, *Database Internals*).
- **Reading Materials**: Store notes, summaries, and clickable reference links to external documentation or video lectures.
- **Pomodoro Study Session**: Start the built-in study timer before you begin reading. When done, log your focused minutes to track cumulative study hours.

---

## 9. Dedicated Examination System (`/examinations`)
Participate in assessments and practice mock certification exams:
- **Exam Catalog**: Browse available exams with duration limits and passing score thresholds.
- **Taking an Examination (`/examinations/take/[id]`)**:
  - The test runner opens in clean focus mode with a live countdown timer.
  - Navigate questions using the interactive question navigator grid.
  - **Autosync**: Your draft answers are automatically saved to the server every 30 seconds.
  - **Proctoring**: Stay in fullscreen mode and avoid switching browser tabs. Focus loss triggers an integrity alert.
  - **Submission**: Click **Submit Examination** when finished.
- **Review Guard & Scorecard (`/examinations/review/[id]`)**:
  - Once submitted, inspect your final score, pass/fail result, and question-by-question explanations.

---

## 10. Productivity & Learning Analytics (`/analytics`)
Evaluate your performance through data-driven insights:
- **Task Velocity**: Analyze your completion rates and distribution across priorities.
- **Study Hours**: Review total hours invested per subject over the last 30 days.
- **Exam Distribution**: Check your examination grade distribution ($85-100\%$, $70-84\%$, $50-69\%$, $<50\%$) and overall pass rate.

---

## 11. Notifications Hub (`/notifications`)
- Stay on top of upcoming due dates with in-app reminders.
- Click **Scan Deadlines** to run an on-demand scan of tasks due within 24 hours and exams within 48 hours.
- Filter notifications by category tabs: `All`, `Deadlines`, `Exams`, `Habits`, and `System`.
